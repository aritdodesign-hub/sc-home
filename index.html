Roof · Siding · Windows

A Ranch Home, Top to Bottom

This white ranch house had a tired roof and worn-out siding. We gave it all three: a new roof, new grey vinyl siding with dark shutters, and new windows, done as one job. Bundling the work is where our biggest savings are.

  • New roof over the whole house
  • New vinyl siding and shutters

Windows · Siding

From Rotting Panels to a Bay Window

Before: three dark, dated windows set in stained panel siding, with moss creeping up the bottom. After: a bright new bay window with white trim, wrapped in clean tan vinyl siding and a new soffit overhead.

  • Rotting panels replaced with vinyl siding

Siding · Carport

A Fresh Face over the Carport

The carport side of this home was the first thing you saw from the driveway, and it showed its age. We wrapped the gable in new tan vinyl siding with a crisp vent, fitted a new window and finished the trim, so the garage view finally matches the rest of the house.

  • New vinyl siding on the gable
  • New window and trim
  • Gable vent set in clean and level
